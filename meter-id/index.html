Meter ID from the number on your meter

For Diehl IZAR water meters with a number like H25XA036488 printed on them: one letter, two digits, two letters, then six digits. The gateway config needs the meter's radio ID, and on these meters it's a different way of writing the same number.

Spaces and dashes are fine.

In the gateway config:


  





If the gateway never shows a reading with this ID, your meter isn't one of these, and you'll need to find its ID with the survey firmware instead. Only meters that wmbusmeters lists with manufacturer SAP carry their number this way.



How it works: the year and the six-digit serial together (25 + 036488 = 25036488) are the ID in hexadecimal (017E06C8). The diameter letter (A) sets the top three bits of it (01 → 21), giving 0x217E06C8. The other two letters aren't part of the ID.
